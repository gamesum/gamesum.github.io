1. Introduction

This Creator Agreement ("Agreement") governs your participation as a content creator ("Creator") on the AFTERGLO marketplace, operated by Ward Legacy Group LLC ("AFTERGLO," "we," "us," or "our"). By uploading content for sale on the AFTERGLO platform or checking the certification checkbox during the upload process, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to this Agreement in full.

This Agreement is incorporated by reference into and made part of AFTERGLO's Terms of Service. In the event of any conflict between this Agreement and the Terms of Service, this Agreement controls with respect to Creator-specific matters.

AFTERGLO reserves the right to modify this Agreement at any time. We will notify Creators of material changes by email. Continued listing of content on the marketplace after any modification constitutes acceptance of the revised Agreement.

2. Eligibility to Sell

To sell Sequences on the AFTERGLO marketplace, you must:

  • Be at least 18 years of age, or be at least 13 years of age with verifiable written consent from a parent or legal guardian.
  • Have a valid, active AFTERGLO account in good standing.
  • Have a valid Stripe account (or be able to create one) to receive payouts. Stripe may impose its own eligibility requirements based on your country of residence.
  • Own, or have secured all necessary rights and licenses to, every element of the content you upload, including FSEQ sequence data and any bundled audio files.
  • Not have had your Creator privileges previously revoked by AFTERGLO for a violation of this Agreement or the Terms of Service.

AFTERGLO reserves the right to approve or deny Creator status at its sole discretion, and to revoke Creator access at any time for violations of this Agreement.

3. Revenue Share

When a buyer purchases your Sequence on the AFTERGLO marketplace, the revenue is split as follows:

Creator receives: 70% of the sale price set for the Sequence.

AFTERGLO retains: 30% as a platform fee, which covers all transaction costs including Stripe payment processing fees, hosting, and marketplace operations. No additional fees are deducted from the Creator's 70%.

You, as the Creator, set the price for your Sequence subject to any minimum or maximum price limits that AFTERGLO may establish. AFTERGLO reserves the right to introduce price floors or ceilings with reasonable advance notice to Creators.

AFTERGLO may, at its discretion, offer promotional discounts or sales on the platform. If a promotional discount is applied to your Sequence, your 70% share is calculated based on the discounted sale price. AFTERGLO will make reasonable efforts to notify Creators before applying platform-wide promotional pricing to their listings.

Free Sequences (priced at $0.00) generate no revenue and are not subject to the revenue share. You may list Sequences as free voluntarily.

4. Payout Schedule

Creator earnings are paid out on a monthly basis, subject to the following conditions:

  • Minimum payout threshold: Your accumulated unpaid earnings must reach at least $10.00 USD before a payout is initiated. If your balance is below $10.00 at the end of a payout period, the balance rolls over to the next month.
  • Payment method: All payouts are made via Stripe. You must have a valid Stripe account connected to your AFTERGLO Creator profile to receive payments. AFTERGLO is not responsible for delays caused by Stripe's processing times or your failure to maintain a valid Stripe account.
  • Payout timing: Payouts are processed within the first 10 business days of each calendar month for earnings accumulated in the prior month, subject to any holds or review periods.
  • Currency: All payouts are made in US dollars (USD). AFTERGLO is not responsible for currency conversion fees or rates charged by your bank or Stripe.
  • Holds: AFTERGLO reserves the right to place a hold on payouts pending investigation of a dispute, DMCA claim, or suspected fraudulent activity. We will notify you promptly if a hold is placed on your account.

To receive payouts, you are responsible for providing accurate payment information and maintaining a valid Stripe account. AFTERGLO is not liable for failed payouts resulting from incorrect or outdated payment information.

7. DMCA Takedown Compliance

AFTERGLO respects intellectual property rights and complies with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA). If AFTERGLO receives a valid DMCA takedown notice alleging that your Sequence infringes a third party's copyright, we will:

  • Promptly remove or disable access to the allegedly infringing Sequence.
  • Notify you of the takedown notice (to the extent not prohibited by law).
  • Provide you with an opportunity to submit a DMCA counter-notification if you believe the takedown was made in error.

To submit a DMCA takedown notice regarding content on the AFTERGLO platform, contact us at wardlegacygroup@gmail.com with "DMCA Takedown" in the subject line. Your notice must include:

  • Identification of the copyrighted work you claim has been infringed.
  • Identification of the allegedly infringing material on our platform (URL or sufficient description).
  • Your contact information (name, address, email, phone number).
  • A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorized by the copyright owner.
  • A statement made under penalty of perjury that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on their behalf.
  • Your physical or electronic signature.

Creators who receive repeated valid DMCA takedowns may have their Creator privileges suspended or permanently revoked.

If you knowingly upload copyrighted content without authorization and AFTERGLO receives a valid DMCA notice, AFTERGLO reserves the right to pursue legal remedies including seeking damages and injunctive relief, following a written warning.

11. Termination

Either party may terminate your Creator status at any time.

You may remove all your listed Sequences and cease selling on the platform at any time through your account settings or by contacting us. Removal of your content from the marketplace will take effect within a reasonable time, but copies already delivered to purchasers remain valid under their existing licenses.

AFTERGLO may terminate your Creator access immediately and without prior notice if you:

  • Violate this Agreement or the Terms of Service.
  • Receive multiple valid DMCA takedown notices.
  • Engage in fraudulent activity or attempt to manipulate sales data, reviews, or payouts.
  • Provide false information in your Creator certification.

Upon termination, any pending earnings that have reached the $10.00 minimum payout threshold will be remitted in the next regular payout cycle, less any applicable offsets for disputed transactions or damages. Earnings below the $10.00 threshold at the time of termination for cause may be forfeited.
