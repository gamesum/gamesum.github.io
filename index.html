The Permanent Advantage

Why Permanent LED Lighting?

Forget the annual ladder climb. AFTERGLO installs a fully integrated system that lives in your roofline — ready whenever you are.

🏠

Never Hang Lights Again

Our fixtures are permanently mounted in your roofline, protected from the elements and invisible during the day. Holidays, game days, and everyday ambiance — always ready.

🌈

16 Million Color Options

Full RGBW LED technology means you can match any mood, holiday, or sports team color with pixel-perfect precision — all from your smartphone.

📱

App-Controlled Anywhere

Schedule scenes, trigger holiday sequences, or change colors on the fly. Our smart controller works with your phone, voice assistants, and home automation.

⚡

Built to Last a Decade

IP65-rated weatherproof fixtures, UV-stable materials, and low-voltage wiring designed to handle Utah's freeze-thaw cycles and intense summer sun.

Simple Process

Up & Running in 3 Steps

We handle everything from design to final walkthrough. Most residential installs are completed in a single day.

1

Free Consultation

We visit your home, measure your roofline, and build a custom lighting plan. You'll see exactly what it will look like — including a color preview — before we touch a thing.

2

Professional Installation

Our licensed crew mounts flush-channel fixtures along your eaves, runs low-voltage wiring, and connects your smart controller. Clean, fast, and guaranteed.

3

Enjoy Forever

We walk you through the app, set up your first scene, and leave you with a system that's backed by a 10-year fixture warranty and lifetime installation support.
