The Hardware

Commercial-Grade Fixtures, Residential Price

Professional components. Built to last. And unlike most LED systems, ours have four color channels — not three.

  • Real Warm White — Not a Fake — most LED systems "mix" colors to fake white, which looks cold and blue. Ours has a dedicated white bulb built in, so warm white actually looks warm
  • Built-In Security Lighting — that same dedicated white channel pulls double duty. Flip it on and your entire roofline floods with bright, clean white light. No separate fixtures, no extra wiring — security lighting that's already installed
