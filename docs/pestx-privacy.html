PestX Coach — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 6, 2026

The privacy of your data — and it is your data, not ours — is a big deal to us. We will never sell your personal information to any third party, period.

PestX ("we", "us") operates the PestX Coach mobile application (the "App"), a sales coaching tool for door-to-door pest control representatives. This Privacy Policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

  • Email address, display name, profile photo (if provided)
  • Sign-in provider (Google, Apple, or email/password)
  • A unique user ID assigned by Firebase Authentication
  • The date your account was created and the last time you opened the App

Recordings and transcripts

  • Audio files you record or upload through the App
  • Text transcripts you paste directly
  • Automated transcripts generated from your audio recordings
  • AI-generated coaching feedback associated with each recording

Usage and security data

  • Recording file size and metadata (filename, upload time)
  • Storage usage totals per account
  • Manager-curated training materials added to the knowledge base
  • IP addresses logged at sign-in for security and fraud prevention (not used for advertising)

We do not collect device contacts, precise location, or other personal information beyond what is described above.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide AI coaching feedback on the sales calls you submit
  • Allow you to review your own past calls and performance score
  • Allow your team manager to review your performance and provide feedback
  • Send push notifications for chat messages, incentives, and team broadcasts
  • Maintain the App and operate the service
  • Enforce usage limits (e.g., storage caps)
  • Investigate and prevent unauthorized access or abuse

3. AI Processing & Your Data

The App uses AI models (Anthropic Claude) to generate coaching feedback from call transcripts. We want to be explicit about how this works:

  • Your transcripts are not used to train AI foundation models. Anthropic processes your data under an enterprise agreement that prohibits using customer data to train or improve their general models.
  • Audio is never sent to Anthropic — only the text transcript is.
  • Your data is isolated from other customers' data at all times.

4. Where Your Data Is Stored

Your data is stored on Google Cloud (Firebase) servers in the United States, operated under Google's infrastructure and policies. Specifically:

  • Audio files are stored in Firebase Cloud Storage, scoped to your user account.
  • Transcripts, coaching feedback, and account metadata are stored in Cloud Firestore.
  • Authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication.

Audio files and transcripts are accessible only to:

  • You (the account holder), and
  • Designated managers within your team (managers can review recordings belonging to reps on their team).

5. Third-Party Services

The App relies on the following third-party services:

Service Purpose Data shared
Firebase (Google Cloud) Auth, storage, database, serverless functions, push notifications Account info, audio files, transcripts, usage data
Google Cloud Speech-to-Text Converting your audio recordings into text transcripts Audio recordings (for transcription only — not retained by Google for training)
Anthropic (Claude API) Generating AI coaching feedback from transcripts Transcripts only (no audio sent; enterprise agreement prohibits training use)
Apple / Google Sign-In Optional third-party authentication Email address and display name from your Apple or Google account

Each provider operates under its own privacy policy. We do not sell your data to any third party.

6. Consent for Recording

You are responsible for ensuring that any audio you upload was recorded in compliance with applicable laws. Some U.S. states (including California, Washington, Florida, Pennsylvania, and others) require all parties to a conversation to consent before being recorded. Do not upload recordings that were captured without the required consent in your jurisdiction.

7. Data Retention

  • Recordings, transcripts, and coaching feedback are retained until you (or a manager) delete them.
  • You can delete any individual recording from the Timeline tab in the App.
  • You can delete your entire account from the account menu (avatar → Delete account). Full instructions and an email-request option are on the Delete Account page. Account deletion removes:
    • All recordings (Firestore + Cloud Storage)
    • Your user profile and coaching history
    • Your Firebase Authentication record
  • Residual data in system backups is purged within 60 days of account closure.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you (visible in-app: account menu and Timeline)
  • Correct your display name or other profile fields
  • Delete individual recordings or your entire account
  • Portability — request an export of your data by emailing us at the address below
  • Object to processing in certain circumstances

California residents may have additional rights under the CCPA, including the right to know what personal information we collect and to opt out of any sale of personal information (we do not sell personal information). To exercise any right, contact us at the address below.

9. Government Requests

We will not disclose your data to government agencies or law enforcement unless we are legally compelled to do so by a valid court order, subpoena, or warrant. Where legally permitted, we will notify you before disclosing your data.

10. Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • All data in transit is encrypted using SSL/TLS.
  • Firebase Security Rules restrict data access so each user can read and write only their own recordings.
  • Only managers (designated by an existing manager) can read other users' recordings or modify the team knowledge base.
  • API keys used for AI processing are stored server-side in Cloud Functions and are never embedded in the App.

No system is perfectly secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately at the address below.

11. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be announced within the App.
