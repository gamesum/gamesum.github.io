Features

Everything your team needs in the field

Built specifically for door-to-door pest control — from the first knock to the year-end bonus check.

🤖
AI Sales Coaching

Record your pitch on the spot or upload audio afterward. Claude AI transcribes the call and delivers instant, actionable coaching tailored to pest control door-knocking.

  • Tone, pacing, and objection-handling feedback
  • Specific line-by-line suggestions
  • Full transcripts saved to your timeline
  • Paste a transcript manually if you prefer
💬
Team Messaging

Stay connected with your crew without leaving the app. Real-time chat built for reps who spend the day on doorsteps.

  • GIF support to celebrate wins
  • Photo and file attachments
  • Push notifications for new messages
  • Manager-to-team broadcast messages
💰
Commission Dashboard

Never wonder what you've earned. The commission dashboard automatically calculates your upfront payout and projects your year-end bonus as accounts accumulate.

  • Upfront commission per account
  • Year-end payout projection
  • Monthly and season totals
  • Always in sync with your recorded sales
📚
Training Knowledge Base

Managers can upload scripts, training materials, and guides directly into the app. Reps always have the latest resources at their fingertips — no group chats, no lost PDFs.

  • Manager-curated content library
  • Sales scripts and rebuttals
  • Product and service reference docs
  • Instantly available to the whole team
How It Works

From doorstep to coaching in seconds

No complicated setup. Record, submit, and improve — all from your phone.

1
Record Your Pitch

Tap record before you knock, or upload audio afterward. You can also paste a transcript directly.

2
AI Transcribes

Google Cloud Speech-to-Text converts your audio into a clean transcript — no manual work required.

3
Get Coaching

Claude AI analyzes the transcript and delivers personalized, specific coaching notes within seconds.

4
Improve & Earn

Apply the feedback, track your accounts, and watch your commission dashboard grow.
