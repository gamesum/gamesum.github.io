About AFTERGLO

Utah's Year-Round Lighting Company.

AFTERGLO is a division of Ward Legacy Group LLC, based in South Jordan, Utah. We design and install permanent RGBW roofline lighting, traditional C9 Christmas string lights, and full-scale, music-synchronized Christmas light shows for homes across the state.

AFTERGLO does two things well. For homeowners who are done with the ladder every November, we install permanent RGBW roofline lighting once — flush-mounted, controlled from your phone, ready for Halloween, the Fourth of July, game day, or the holidays whenever you want it. For homeowners who want the classic, hand-hung Christmas look without doing it themselves, we run a full-service traditional C9 string light program: we install before Thanksgiving, service the lights through the season, and take it all down after New Year's.

Alongside both, we're the only company bringing full-scale, music-synchronized Christmas light shows to residential yards in Utah: pixel-addressable LED arches, giant trees, and candy cane props choreographed to music and run from the same app as your roofline.

We're licensed and insured statewide, and every job — whether it's a single roofline, a seasonal string light install, or a full yard show — gets the same standard: clean installation, real communication, and a system built to outlast the season.
