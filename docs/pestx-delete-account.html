PestX Coach — Delete Your Account & Data

App: PestX Coach
Developer: PestX
Last updated: May 26, 2026

This page explains how to permanently delete your PestX Coach account and all data associated with it.

Option 1: Delete from inside the app (instant)

The fastest way:

  1. Open the PestX Coach app.
  2. Sign in to the account you want to delete.
  3. Tap your profile avatar in the top-right corner.
  4. Tap Delete account.
  5. Confirm. Your account and all data are removed within seconds.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you no longer have access to the app or prefer not to delete it yourself, email us:

office@pestx.org

Include:

  • The email address associated with your PestX Coach account
  • A short note stating that you want your account and data deleted

We will process your request and confirm by email within 7 business days.

What gets deleted

When you delete your account, the following is permanently removed:

  • Your Firebase Authentication record (you can no longer sign in)
  • Your user profile (display name, photo, role, storage settings)
  • Every audio recording you uploaded or recorded, from Cloud Storage
  • Every transcript generated from your recordings, from Firestore
  • Every coaching response generated for your recordings, from Firestore
  • The chat history between you and the AI coach for each recording

What gets kept

Nothing user-identifying is retained. We do not keep backup copies of your recordings, transcripts, or profile after deletion.

The only exception: aggregate, fully anonymous usage statistics from Google Cloud (e.g., total bytes stored in the project, total function invocations) may remain in Google's analytics dashboards. These cannot be linked back to you.

Retention period

Zero retention. Deletion is immediate and irreversible. There is no grace period or recovery window — once you confirm deletion, the data is gone.
