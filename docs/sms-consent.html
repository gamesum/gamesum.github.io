Text Message Consent

AFTERGLO Lighting, operated by Ward Legacy Group LLC · South Jordan, Utah · Effective October 9, 2026

This page explains every way a homeowner can agree to receive text messages from AFTERGLO Lighting, the exact wording they agree to, and how we record it. AFTERGLO sends two kinds of texts, and each needs its own consent:

  • Customer care texts: the homeowner's quote request, estimate, consultation, appointment scheduling, installation updates, and customer service.
  • Marketing texts: occasional seasonal lighting offers, promotions, and installation discounts. Written consent only, never verbal.

Texting is always optional. Consent is not a condition of getting a quote or buying anything from AFTERGLO.

1. Website quote forms

Every quote form on afterglolighting.org (for example afterglolighting.org/landing.html and afterglolighting.org/contact.html) has two separate checkboxes next to the submit button. Both start unchecked, and the form submits whether or not either is checked.

Checkbox 1: customer care texts (optional, unchecked) Text me about my quote and appointments. I agree to automated customer care texts from AFTERGLO Lighting about my quote, appointments, installation and service.
Checkbox 2: marketing texts (optional, unchecked) Text me seasonal offers. I agree to automated marketing texts from AFTERGLO Lighting about promotions and discounts.

Directly below both boxes: "Optional, and not required to get a quote or buy. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out," with links to our SMS Terms and Privacy Policy. When a box is checked we store its exact wording and the submission time with the request. When it is not checked, we do not text that number for that purpose.

2. Facebook and Instagram lead forms

Lead forms in our Facebook and Instagram ads use the same two optional, unchecked checkboxes with the same wording as our website. A homeowner who submits without checking either box is contacted by phone call or email only.

3. Verbal consent (in person or by phone)

Our representatives meet homeowners at their door, at scheduled consultations, and by phone. Before we text a number collected this way, the representative reads this script word for word:

Representative "Can AFTERGLO Lighting send you text messages about your lighting quote, appointment scheduling, and installation updates? Message frequency varies and message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for assistance. Our Terms and Privacy Policy are on our website at afterglolighting.org. Do you agree to receive these texts?"
Example homeowner response "Yes, that's fine."

Only a clear yes counts. If the homeowner says no, hesitates, or wants to think about it, we do not text them, and we may still call if they ask us to. Verbal consent covers customer care texts only. It is never used for marketing texts, which require the written checkbox above.

Immediately after a yes, the representative confirms it back to the homeowner and records:

Phone numberThe mobile number the homeowner agreed to receive texts at
DateThe date consent was given
Affirmative responseThe homeowner's answer, for example "Yes, that's fine."
Employee nameThe representative who read the script
Consent methodVerbal, in person, or verbal, by phone

No text is sent until this record exists. Every representative who collects phone numbers is trained on this procedure, reads the script as written every time, and treats any doubt about consent as no consent.

Opting out and getting help

  • Reply STOP to any message to stop all texts. We send one confirmation and nothing after that. Reply START to opt back in.
  • Reply HELP for assistance, or call (385) 337 4144 or email afterglolights@gmail.com.
  • Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  • No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties.
