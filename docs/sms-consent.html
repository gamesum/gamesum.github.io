Verbal SMS Consent Procedure

AFTERGLO Lighting (Afterglo Permanent Lighting), a division of Ward Legacy Group LLC · South Jordan, Utah · Effective October 9, 2026

When this procedure applies

AFTERGLO talks with homeowners in person (at their door or at a scheduled consultation) and by phone. Before we send any text message to a number collected in one of those conversations, our representative must get the homeowner's verbal consent using the script below and record it. Homeowners who request a quote online agree to texts on the quote form instead (see Other ways homeowners opt in).

The script

Our representative reads this to the homeowner word for word:

Representative "Can Afterglo Permanent Lighting send you text messages about your lighting quote, appointment scheduling, and installation updates? Message frequency varies and message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for assistance. Our Terms and Privacy Policy are available on our website. Do you agree to receive these texts?"
Example homeowner response "Yes, that's fine."

Only a clear yes counts as consent. If the homeowner says no, hesitates, or asks to think about it, the representative does not record consent and AFTERGLO does not text that number. The representative may still answer questions or follow up by phone call if the homeowner asks for that.

What we record

Immediately after the homeowner agrees, the representative confirms the consent back to them and records the following in our lead management system:

Phone numberThe mobile number the homeowner agreed to receive texts at
DateThe date consent was given
Affirmative responseThe homeowner's answer, for example "Yes, that's fine."
Employee nameThe representative who read the script
Consent methodVerbal, in person or verbal, by phone

No text is sent to a number until this record exists.

What verbal consent covers

  • Verbal consent is used only for informational and customer service messages: the homeowner's lighting quote, appointment scheduling, and installation updates.
  • Promotional or marketing messages require a separate written opt-in. Verbal consent is never used for them.
  • Consent to texts is not a condition of buying anything from AFTERGLO.

Opting out and getting help

  • Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. We confirm the opt-out and send nothing further.
  • Reply HELP for assistance, or call (385) 337 4144 or email afterglolights@gmail.com.
  • Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies.
  • Mobile information is not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Other ways homeowners opt in

Homeowners who request a quote on our website agree to texts through the consent statement shown next to the form's submit button, for example at afterglolighting.org/landing.html and afterglolighting.org/contact.html. We store the exact consent wording they saw and when they submitted it.

Staff requirements

  • Every AFTERGLO representative who collects phone numbers is trained on this procedure before working with homeowners.
  • The script is read as written, every time. Representatives do not paraphrase it or skip the STOP and HELP language.
  • A representative who is unsure whether consent was given treats it as not given.
