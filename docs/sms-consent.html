Text Message Consent

AFTERGLO Lighting, operated by Ward Legacy Group LLC · South Jordan, Utah · Effective November 10, 2026

This page explains how a homeowner agrees to receive text messages from AFTERGLO Lighting, the exact wording they agree to, and how we record it. AFTERGLO sends customer care texts only: messages about the homeowner's quote request, estimate, appointment scheduling, installation updates, and customer service.

The only way to opt in is the optional, unchecked checkbox on our website quote forms. Texting is always optional. Consent is not a condition of getting a quote or buying anything from AFTERGLO.

Website quote forms

Every quote form on afterglolighting.org (for example afterglolighting.org/landing.html and afterglolighting.org/contact.html) has one text message checkbox next to the submit button. It starts unchecked, and the form submits whether or not it is checked.

Checkbox: customer care texts (optional, unchecked) Text me about my quote and appointments. I agree to automated customer care texts from AFTERGLO Lighting about my quote, appointments, installation and service.

Directly below the checkbox: "Optional, and not required to get a quote or buy. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out," with links to our SMS Terms and Privacy Policy. When the box is checked we store its exact wording and the submission time with the request. When it is not checked, we do not text that number, and we contact the homeowner by phone call or email only.

Messages we send

  • Confirmation and follow up on a quote request or estimate
  • Appointment scheduling, reminders, and changes
  • Installation updates
  • Customer service and replies to the homeowner's questions

We do not send marketing or promotional text messages.

Opting out and getting help

  • Reply STOP to any message to stop all texts. We send one confirmation and nothing after that. Reply START to opt back in.
  • Reply HELP for assistance, or call (385) 337 4144 or email afterglolights@gmail.com.
  • Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  • No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.
