Specifications

Power in
24 V DC with reverse polarity protection, MP2315 buck and AMS1117 regulator
Network
Wired Ethernet first, Wi-Fi as backup. Rev 1 drew a LAN8720A RMII PHY, but the ESP32-S3 has no Ethernet MAC, so the next revision moves to a W5500 over SPI.
LED outputs
4 channels, RGBW, 5 V logic through 74AHCT125 level shifters
Data
RS485 via SP3485
Display
SSD1306 OLED status screen, 128 × 32 on rev 1, going to 128 × 64 for a real settings screen
Flashing
USB-C through CP2102N
Reliability
TPL5010 hardware watchdog
Firmware
Custom WLED fork, remote updates disabled by design
Design files
KiCad 9, four A3 hierarchical sheets (power, MCU and comms, signal outputs, breakout), each generated by a Python script. ERC clean.

On the box

Why build a controller

A lot of AFTERGLO's work is replacing controllers that failed on someone else's install. Off-the-shelf boards lean on Wi-Fi, lose their place on a flaky network, and give the installer nothing to look at when something goes wrong.

This board puts a cable first, shows its status on the front, and has a hardware watchdog that restarts it if the firmware hangs. A pro tier with onboard SD storage for longer shows is planned.
