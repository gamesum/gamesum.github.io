Puck variants

DM Puck
The hub. Talks to the DM's phone over BLE, relays to player pucks over ESP-NOW, and holds turn order.
Premium Screen Puck
Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-2.1, 2.1-inch round 480 × 480 touch display, QMI8658 IMU, 32-LED WS2812 ring, vibration motor, 1800 mAh LiPo
Budget Screen Puck
1.28-inch round GC9A01 touch display, with the same ring, motor, and battery
Counter Puck
ESP32-C3 Super Mini, TM1637 4-digit display, two buttons, SK9822 LED ring, piezo beeper, 2000 mAh LiPo with TP4056 charging. About $16.65 a unit.
Battery life
20+ hours on the Counter Puck, 4 to 5 hours active on the screen pucks

On the table

Health
Ring color runs green to red as HP drops, a slow red heartbeat at 0, dark when dead
Turns
Solid blue and a buzz on your turn, flashing white when you're next
Hits

App

Flutter, iOS and Android
Encounters
Player groups, a monster library, and an encounter builder that sorts initiative

Solo mode
A health tracker that works without an encounter
Design
Dark Dungeon theme: glass cards, gold gradients, Cinzel and Spectral type, pulsing turn rings
Tests
53+ automated tests. Runs on a Pixel 9 Pro.

Firmware

Rule
No game logic on the puck. The app owns the game; the puck only runs hardware.
Wire format
Plain text commands such as CMD=TURN_START color=#4A86FF vibrate=1, behind a 15-byte header
Why
The app's debug log shows the exact bytes on the wire, so the simulator and the real puck speak one language
Compatibility
Unknown commands are logged and ignored, so an older puck with a newer app degrades instead of breaking
Bring-up
Boots on the Waveshare 2.1 board with a hand-written ST7701 display driver. A real BLE link to the app is confirmed; overlapping writes that dropped the connection now go through a queue.
Flashing
Python/Tkinter desktop flasher, and a single-file Web Serial browser flasher
Enclosures
Printed by Rocket Forge
