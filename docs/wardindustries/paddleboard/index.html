Specifications

Board
Inflatable SUP
Motors
Two D5065 400KV outrunners in oil-filled PETG pods with double-lip shaft seals
ESCs
Two Flycolor 150A waterproof marine ESCs, water-cooled by a 24 V diaphragm pump
Battery
6S LiPo packs wired in parallel
Radio
ESP32-WROOM-32 on both ends, linked by ESP-NOW. No router, no pairing screen.
Design
Onshape

Handheld transmitter

Throttle
Hall-effect thumb throttle
Screen
SSD1306 OLED
Power
18650 cell with TP4056 charging
Charging port
IP67 USB-C bulkhead, power only
Housing
Sealed

Firmware

Builds
TX in the handheld remote, RX in the deck box driving the ESCs by servo PWM
Protocol
CRC-checked packets. Host tests reject all 80 single-bit corruptions and map throttle exactly to 1000, 1500, and 2000 µs.
Failsafe
The receiver cuts throttle when valid packets stop, and the remote shows it
Bench bring-up
August 2026. Four bugs found and fixed on hardware: a display check that couldn't see a missing screen, a display fault that froze the remote at boot, a link readout that stayed healthy during failsafe, and a remote that couldn't wake from sleep.
Sleep
The ULP wake was dropped for a 2-second timer poll: a few mA more, but no chance of a dead throttle
