About the shop

Ward Industries is where Shane Ward keeps the things he builds. Most of it starts as a problem at work: a lighting controller that kept failing, a sales team that needed a better map, a truck that needed a dashboard.

The work runs from circuit boards drawn in KiCad to apps running on phones, with the enclosures printed in-house.

Hardware
ESP32 family, Arduino, KiCad 9, custom PCBs
Software
Flutter, Firebase, ESP-IDF, PlatformIO, TypeScript, Raspberry Pi / FPP
Fabrication
3D printing, Onshape, soldering, upholstery
