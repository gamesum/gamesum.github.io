Send Show to Your Controller
This sends this show directly to your AFTERGLO controller over your home Wi-Fi. You only need to do this once per show.
Not sure what this is?
Option 1 — Check your controller's screen: When your AFTERGLO controller starts up, a number like 192.168.1.105 appears on its screen. Type that number here.

Option 2 — Check your router: Type 192.168.1.1 into your browser address bar. Look for a page with a list of connected devices and find "AFTERGLO."

Can't find it? Use the "Download Show File" button instead — then drag that file onto your controller's web page.
💡 Tip: Make sure your phone or computer is connected to your home Wi-Fi (not mobile data) before sending.
